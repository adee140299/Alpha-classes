📖 About Us – Alpha Classes

Alpha Classes is a trusted and dedicated educational institute founded by Amarnath Yadav (B.Sc, B.Ed), a retired BMC school teacher with over 26 years of teaching experience. Built on the values of quality education, personal attention, and consistent growth, Alpha Classes aims to shape the academic journey of students from Class 1 to 12 (Science) with strong fundamentals and confidence. We provide coaching for Hindi Medium, Semi-English, and English Medium students across SSC, CBSE, and ICSE boards. Our classroom environment is nurturing and disciplined, with a focus on conceptual clarity, regular assessments, and individual guidance.

👨‍🏫 Why Choose Alpha Classes?

✅ Experienced Faculty: Learn from a teacher who has taught thousands of students during his 26+ year career.

✅ All Subjects under One Roof: From basic subjects in primary classes to core Science subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Math) in higher secondary.

✅ Small Batch Sizes: Ensures individual attention and better doubt resolution.

✅ All Mediums Supported: Hindi, Semi-English, and English mediums are welcomed and taught with ease.

✅ Strong Foundation for Future Exams: Early foundation for competitive exams like JEE, NEET, and school Olympiads.

✅ Moral & Academic Guidance: Focus on building discipline, responsibility, and goal-setting alongside academic growth.
