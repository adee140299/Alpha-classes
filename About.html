About Alpha Classes

Mumbai's trusted academic coaching center led by veteran BMC-retired educators with 26+ years of proven teaching experience.

26+ Years of Excellence

Building Strong Futures with Conceptual Clarity

Alpha Classes was established by Amarnath Yadav (B.Sc, B.Ed), a dedicated BMC school teacher with over 26 years of teaching experience. Rooted in values of quality education, personal empathy, and consistent discipline, Alpha Classes shapes the academic journey of students from Class 1 to 12 (Science).

We provide specialized batches for Hindi Medium, Semi-English, and English Medium students across Maharashtra SSC, CBSE, and ICSE boards. Our classrooms maintain small batch limits so no child is left behind.

Why Parents Trust Us:

  • 26+ Years BMC Experience: Decades of insight into board exam marking schemes and student psychology.
  • All Mediums Supported: Complete support for Hindi, Semi-English, and English medium students.
  • Personalized Attention: Strictly monitored batch sizes to enable immediate doubt resolution.
  • Transparent Tracking: Real-time attendance and fee receipts via our dedicated Student Portal.
